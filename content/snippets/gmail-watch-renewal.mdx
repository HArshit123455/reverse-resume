## Context

A Gmail `users.watch` expires after seven days, sends no warning, and has no PATCH: re-arming is the same call again. Microsoft Graph at least emits lifecycle events; Gmail just goes silent. So a scheduled job is the only thing between a mailbox and a week-long push outage. Push also sits behind a feature flag, and mailboxes connected while it was off needed a way to join without reconnecting.

## What this proves

- Renewing a full day early on a six-hourly tick gives four chances before any watch lapses.
- The second pass turns a feature flag into a real switch: operators can enable push without asking any customer to reconnect.
- The push endpoint trusts only Pub/Sub's OIDC token (valid Google signature, the expected service account, verified email) and deliberately ignores the notification's `historyId`, so a replayed or reordered push can never rewind the cursor.
